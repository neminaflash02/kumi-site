Safety

A women-only space, seriously protected.

  • Selfie verificationA person on the team reviews the selfie, not an algorithm. Without the badge you can't see where and when events happen.
  • Block and reportWhoever you block disappears on both sides, chat included. Three reports and the account is suspended.
  • Your data stays yoursNobody sees your surname or date of birth. The account can be deleted in one tap, with everything in it.
