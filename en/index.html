Inside the app.

Kumi's real screens, with the demo profiles.

Made for friendship.

Kumi was made for meeting other women and doing activities together: drinks, an exhibition, a walk, a concert. It's not a dating app: here you look for friends.

Every profile is verified with a selfie, checked by our team. Without the badge you can't see where and when events happen.

You can suggest events yourselves, or find them ready in your city. Every event has a set number of places, so the group stays small and you really get to know each other.

Got an idea or a question? Write to us: the Kumi team will reply.
