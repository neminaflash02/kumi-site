Inside the app.

Kumi's real screens, with the demo profiles.

It's not a dating app.

Kumi is for friendships. You join to meet women to do things with, not to find a partner. Anyone looking for something else has no place here.

That's why every profile is verified with a selfie, and the selfie is checked by a person, not an algorithm. Without the badge you can't see where and when events happen.

You can suggest events yourselves, or find them ready in your city: concerts, exhibitions, museums to visit together. Every event has a set number of places, so the group stays small.

If you have an idea or a question, write to us: we answer ourselves, not an autoresponder.
