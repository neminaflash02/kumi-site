How it works.

It's not a dating app: it's made for making friends and actually going out.

  1. Create your profilePhotos, interests and the things you love doing.
  2. Find women like youSee women in your city and how much you have in common.
  3. Start chattingIf you both like each other, a chat opens, with photos, GIFs and voice notes.
  4. Go out togetherConcerts, exhibitions, museums, an aperitivo or a coffee. Or create your own event, with just a few spots.
