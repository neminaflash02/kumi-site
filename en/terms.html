Kumi is an app designed for women: it helps you meet like-minded women, make friends and find good things to do together, from the chat to real life. It is not a dating app and it is not a place to sell or advertise.

3. Who can use Kumi

4. Profile verification and events

  • If you change your main photo, you need to verify again.
  • We may refuse or remove verification if the details don't match or if the Community Guidelines are not respected.
